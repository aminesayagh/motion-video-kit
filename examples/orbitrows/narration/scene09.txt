Think of it this way. Your AI is the planner: it understands your goal and decides what to find out. OrbitRows is its hands on your data. Each request it receives is turned into exact, checked SQL.

Say you ask your AI: "Prepare a new campaign; tell me what's working." The AI doesn't guess from memory. It asks OrbitRows: "show the quantity sold per product over the last ninety days"; "which products were never sold?"; "which variants have less stock than their reorder point?". Each answer comes back exact, and the AI builds the campaign on facts. When OrbitRows isn't sure what a request means, it asks, and the AI passes the question to you.

The same engine can sit inside a product. Your users manage their own records by typing what they want, with the same checks: questions when unsure, changes checked against the structure, undo until they save.
